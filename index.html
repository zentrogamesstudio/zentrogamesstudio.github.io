How it plays

  1. Multiply your army

    Pick the right gate. Multiplying and adding gates grow your crowd, traps and minus gates shrink it.

  2. A real battle at the end

    Your crowd meets the enemy army. Place your squads before the fight, then attack, defend or retreat.

  3. Build your village

    Upgrade the barracks, hospital and blacksmith, and unlock new soldier types.

  4. Duel a friend

    Play online from phone to phone, without a game server.

  5. Seven languages

    English, Turkish, Spanish, French, German, Russian and Azerbaijani.

About Zentro Games

Zentro Games is an independent game studio based in Azerbaijan. We make mobile games that are easy to pick up and have enough depth to keep you playing.

  • No ads, no account

    Multiply Mayhem has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your phone.

  • Built with players

    We test with real players and tune the game from what they tell us.

  • Plays anywhere

    The single-player game works offline. Online duels connect two phones directly, with no game server.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Zentro Games?

An independent game studio based in Azerbaijan. We make mobile games. Multiply Mayhem is our current game.

Which platforms is Multiply Mayhem on?

Android. It is coming soon to Google Play.

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed. The single-player game works offline. Online duels connect two phones directly: over the internet, the same Wi-Fi, Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth.

What data do you collect?

None. Your progress is saved on your device only. The Privacy Policy in the footer has the details.

When will it be released?

We do not have a date to share yet. We will announce it on this site.

How do I delete my progress?

Open Settings in the game and choose Reset progress.

Can we work together?

Yes. Write to us and tell us about your idea.
