What we stand for

  1. Easy to pick up

    One finger, one idea at a time. You understand a Zentro game in seconds, and there is more to find the longer you play.

  2. Honest by default

    Our first game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no account. We tell you what a game does, and nothing more.

  3. Tested with players

    We watch real players, listen to what they say and tune the game before it ships.

  4. Yours to keep

    Your progress stays on your phone. A backup code moves it to another phone, with no account.
