Privacy Policy

Multiply Mayhem (Android app and browser version). Last updated: 19 November 2026.

This policy is published by Zentro Games ("we"). Contact: zentrogames.studio@gmail.com.

Short version

Multiply Mayhem has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run a game server. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, gems, skins, village, settings, language) and, if you use online play, your player name and your last connection settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us. You can erase them at any time (see "Deleting your data").

Online play (internet)

Online matches run directly between two phones (peer to peer). The players exchange a connection code themselves, for example by messaging each other. To find a route between the phones, your device asks Google's public STUN servers (stun.l.google.com and stun1.l.google.com) for its public network address. This reveals your IP address to Google, under Google's own privacy policy. Your opponent's device also receives your IP address, your player name and the match data (such as squad orders, emojis and chat messages). We never see or store any of this.

Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth play (Nearby)

Local play uses the Nearby Connections feature of Google Play services. To discover and connect to a friend's phone, Android may ask for these permissions: Bluetooth (scan, advertise, connect), Nearby Wi-Fi devices, Wi-Fi state, and, on Android 12 and older only, location. Android requires the location permission on those versions for device discovery. The game does not read, use or store your location. Data travels directly from phone to phone.

Other permissions

Vibration is used for haptic feedback and can be turned off in Settings. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Cookies and local storage

The app and the browser version set no cookies and use no tracking technologies. Local storage is used only to keep your save data, which is strictly necessary for the game to work. For that reason no cookie banner is shown. The browser version loads the three.js library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com and two fonts from Google Fonts, so those services can see your IP address when you open it. The Android app includes its libraries and fonts and does not need them.

Children

Multiply Mayhem is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

All data lives on your device. In the app, open Settings and choose RESET PROGRESS to erase your progress and your saved online name. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings erases everything as well. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Your rights

Since we do not collect or hold personal data about you, requests to access, correct or delete it cannot apply to us. If you have a question or believe something in this policy is wrong, write to zentrogames.studio@gmail.com.

Changes

If the app starts to collect any data, for example through a future feature, we will update this policy and the in-app notice before that version is released.

Online oyun (internet)

Online maçlar iki telefon arasında doğrudan (eşler arası) çalışır. Oyuncular bağlantı kodunu kendileri paylaşır, örneğin birbirlerine mesajla. Arkadaşının telefonunu bulmak ve bağlanmak için Android şu izinleri isteyebilir: Bluetooth (tarama, yayın, bağlanma), Yakındaki Wi-Fi cihazları, Wi-Fi durumu ve yalnızca Android 12 ve eski sürümlerde konum. Android bu sürümlerde cihaz keşfi için konum iznini zorunlu tutar. Oyun konumunu okumaz, kullanmaz ve saklamaz. Veri telefondan telefona doğrudan akar.
