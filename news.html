No ads in the first release

Multiply Mayhem launches without ads, without in-app purchases and without an account.

Your progress is saved on your phone. A backup code in Settings lets you move it to another phone whenever you like.

November 2026Game

Online duels, phone to phone

Two players connect directly, so there is no game server.

Duels unlock at level 10. Connect with a shared code over the internet or the same Wi-Fi, or use Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth on Android. Pick a game mode, choose the battlefield and chat with emoji during the match.
