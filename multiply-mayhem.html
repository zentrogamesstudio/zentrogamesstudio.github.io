Multiply Mayhem

In short

  1. Multiply your army

    Pick the right gate. Multiplying and adding gates grow your crowd, traps and minus gates shrink it.

  2. A real battle at the end

    Your crowd meets the enemy army. Place your squads before the fight, then attack, defend or retreat.

  3. Build your village

    Upgrade the barracks, hospital and blacksmith, and unlock new soldier types.

  4. Duel a friend

    Play online from phone to phone, without a game server.

  5. Seven languages

    English, Turkish, Spanish, French, German, Russian and Azerbaijani.

In detail

01

Run, steer, multiply

Drag to steer your crowd through the gates. Multiplying and adding gates make the crowd bigger, traps and minus gates make it smaller. Pull your finger down to brake, and double-tap for a speed boost.

  • Five worlds: Sunny Meadow, Desert Rush, Frozen Peaks, Candy Land and Night Volcano.
  • A chest waits every third level, and an epic chest every fifth.
  • Earn one to three stars on every level.

02

A real battle at the finish line

Your crowd meets the enemy army. Before the fight you place your squads on the field, then you attack, defend or retreat.

  • Eight enemy types, listed in the Enemy Book.
  • A boss guards every fifth level.

03

Choose your formation

Your crowd can run as a loose swarm or in a shape that gives it an edge. Each shape has its own bonus.

  • Phalanx: fewer losses from obstacles.
  • Spearhead: more running speed.
  • Ring: wider pickup reach.
  • Diamond: stronger soldiers.

04

Build your village

After level 5 your village opens. Upgrade its buildings, train new soldier types and make your army stronger between levels.

  • Eleven buildings: Barracks, Hospital, Blacksmith, Shield Hut, Archery Range, Gunner Camp, Mage Tower, Tank Factory, Meeting Room, Gold Mine and Gem Mine.
  • Six soldier types: swordsmen, archers, gunners, mages, shield soldiers and tanks.

05

Duel a friend

Online duels unlock at level 10. There is no game server: the two phones connect directly to each other.

Two game modes

Crowd Runner and Direct Fight. In Direct Fight your squads march in formation and meet the enemy head on, and you can split a squad in two mid-fight. Armies range from 500 to 4,000 troops.

Ways to connect

A shared code over the internet or the same Wi-Fi. Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth connect nearby Android phones with no internet. Bluetooth is slower, so the battle updates less often.

During the match

Choose the battlefield, chat with emoji and surrender if the fight turns against you.

06

Rewards and extras

Daily missions, achievements and a five-day login reward give you a reason to come back.

  • Lucky Spin, with the odds shown in the game.
  • Skins that change how your crowd looks.
  • Six music sets: the default, Arcade, Chill, Epic, Synth and Folk.

07

Your progress is yours

No account is needed. Your progress is saved on your phone. In Settings, Get code gives you a backup code, and Enter code on any phone brings your progress back. Reset progress clears it.
