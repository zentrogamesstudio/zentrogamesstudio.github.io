The studio and the game

Who is Zentro Games?

An independent game studio based in Azerbaijan. We make mobile games. Multiply Mayhem is our current game.

Which platforms is Multiply Mayhem on?

Android. It is coming soon to Google Play.

When will it be released?

We do not have a date to share yet. We will announce it on this site.

Can we work together?

Yes. Write to us and tell us about your idea.

Which languages does the game support?

English, Turkish, Spanish, French, German, Russian and Azerbaijani.

Playing

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed. The single-player game works offline. Online duels connect two phones directly: over the internet, the same Wi-Fi, Wi-Fi Direct or Bluetooth.

How do I move my progress to another phone?

Open Settings and tap Get code to see your backup code, then copy it. On the other phone, open Settings, tap Enter code and paste it. The code replaces the progress on that phone. It works without an account or a server.

When do online duels unlock?

At level 10. Until then you play the single-player levels, which also work offline.

Can I duel someone on a different network?

Yes. A shared code works over the internet, so you do not need to be on the same Wi-Fi. Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth are for phones that are close to each other, and only on Android.

Why does the game ask for a Nearby devices or Bluetooth permission?

Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth play use your phone's Nearby feature. Android may ask for Nearby devices, Bluetooth or location permission so it can find your friend. We never read or store your location.

Data and support

What data do you collect?

None. Your progress is saved on your device only. The Privacy Policy in the footer has the details.

How do I delete my progress?

Open Settings in the game and choose Reset progress.

How do I report a bug?

Write to us. Tell us what happened, which phone you use and the version shown in the corner of the main menu, next to the game name.
