About Zentro Games

Zentro Games is an independent game studio based in Azerbaijan. We make mobile games that are easy to pick up and have enough depth to keep you playing.

We make games for people who play in short sessions on their phone: on the bus, in a queue or on the sofa.

Two things matter to us: a game should be clear in the first seconds, and it should still be worth playing a month later.

How we make a game

  1. Prototype

    We start with something playable and ask one question: is it fun in the first ten seconds?

  2. Player tests

    We watch real players and note where they get stuck, confused or bored.

  3. Tuning

    Gate values, enemy strength and rewards are adjusted until a level feels fair.

  4. Release

    We release it plainly: the store page says what the game really does, and the game does only that.

What guides us

  • No ads, no account

    Multiply Mayhem has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your phone.

  • Built with players

    We test with real players and tune the game from what they tell us.

  • Plays anywhere

    The single-player game works offline. Online duels connect two phones directly, with no game server.
